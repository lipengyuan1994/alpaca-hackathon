# Falsification conditions

Reject or demote the opening-range-breakout candidate if next-observation
execution removes the edge, the result is only the opening print, only one
calendar episode explains it, it duplicates the intraday-continuation or
gap-continuation family without incremental portfolio evidence, or base/severe
option proxy costs erase it. Removal tests for the volume and VWAP
confirmations run one at a time, alongside opening-gap-size slices,
next-observation timing, and family-overlap attribution. Promotion
additionally fails on any shared Group B sample, concentration, coverage,
drawdown, indicative-quote, lineage, or independent-reproduction gate in the
packet.
