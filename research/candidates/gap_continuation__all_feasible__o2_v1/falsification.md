# Falsification conditions

Reject or demote the gap-continuation candidate if apparent gaps are
adjustment artifacts, one direction or date dominates, the continuation or
VWAP confirmation adds no stable evidence, or next-observation execution
removes the result. Publish positive/negative-gap, gap-size, volatility,
macro-date concentration, and first-hour-confirmation slices. Remove the
continuation and VWAP confirmations one at a time only as prescribed
falsifications. Promotion additionally fails on any shared Group B sample,
concentration, coverage, drawdown, indicative-quote, lineage, or
independent-reproduction gate in the packet.
